# CS2 Lootbox v1.1.0

## Music kits

- Added support for **101 music kits** and **1,563 tracks**, supplied through optional music resource packs.
- Added a **CS2-style listening screen** with cover artwork, track selection, playback progress, Previous/Next, Play/Stop and Pause/Resume controls.
- Save a selected track to the kit and play it in a **vanilla jukebox**. Playback starts from the beginning, and ejecting the disc returns your music kit with its saved selection.
- Music volume follows the **Jukebox/Note Blocks** slider. Supplied tracks use mono audio for distance attenuation.
- Kits use a 3D sleeve-and-record model when a matching atlas is available, or their original 2D artwork otherwise.

## Resource packs

- Music audio and artwork are kept outside the mod JAR. Shared model geometry remains in the mod.
- Choose individual kit packs or the **Full Collection** ZIP to install all kits together through the CurseForge app.
- Kits with missing required resources are hidden from creative tabs and cannot open the listening screen. Existing items and saved selections are preserved when a pack is disabled.
- Resource-pack changes refresh kit availability without changing registered item IDs.

## Performance and fixes

- Improved rendering for eligible static cases held in first person by reusing their poses and avoiding empty buffer flushes, while preserving model and texture quality.
- Restored patch-envelope animation after mesh reduction.
- Fixed corrupted text symbols, including **★**, **™**, **•** and **♥**.

## For pack creators

- Added KubeJS music-kit registration and editing with customizable artwork, tracks and model transforms.
- Updated the README and examples for music kits, shared rarity grades, static model items, independent carousel/reveal transforms and collection-image scaling.
- Expanded Creative-mode render diagnostics to help investigate held-case performance.

**Music setup:** Install CS2 Lootbox v1.1.0+ and its dependencies, then enable your music ZIP through **Options > Resource Packs**. Dedicated servers do not need the music resource packs; clients and servers still need matching mod versions and KubeJS definitions.
